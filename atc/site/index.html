Sources, methods, and caveats (click to expand) Take-home is an approximation: single filer, 2026 federal brackets with standard deduction, 7.65% FICA, rough state and city rates at about $60K, and trainee pay. It excludes FERS retirement contributions, health premiums, food, car, and utilities, so treat "left after rent" as an upper bound. Filters exclude rows with missing data when that filter is set.Median household income is the city's 2020–2024 figure from Census QuickFacts, in 2024 dollars. It's household income, not salary, because the Census doesn't publish median salary by city. Dulles uses Loudoun County, Andrews uses Prince George's County, and Teterboro has no figure. Worst safety grade means the lowest safety grade you'll accept. Political lean is the average of the 2020 and 2024 presidential margins in the airport's county (20+ points = hard). Counties are a rough proxy for the metro area around each airport. Fit score: each category is percentile-ranked across the 50 facilities, then combined using your slider weights; "scored %" shows how much of your weight had data for that facility. A fit score is hidden when less than 60% of the weight has data, so facilities with missing rent don't rank on partial information. Strava links open the global heatmap centered on each airport; the heatmap can't be scored, so it's a visual check only. Training success is 123ATC's "Training Success Rate (Avg)", taken from each facility's page (the latest month shown there, August to October 2026). The site doesn't define the base or the class size, so treat it as a rough pass-rate proxy. Small facilities can swing to 100% or 0% on a few trainees; new hires typically run lower than transfers. Trail and park counts come from OpenStreetMap within a 10-mile circle of each airport. They're raw totals, so metro areas score higher, and the circle can include parks across county lines. *Safety grade is derived here, not a published grade: violent crime per 100k of A under 150, B 150–299, C 300–499, D 500–799, F 800+. The national violent crime rate is roughly 360–400 per 100k. Crime: FBI 2024 figures via Wikipedia's city table for large cities; NeighborhoodScout 2024 for smaller cities. Some areas use a nearby proxy (Sterling for Dulles, Clinton MD for Andrews, Warrenton for Potomac TRACON, NYC for JFK and LaGuardia, Atlanta for Atlanta TRACON). Memphis is flagged by the FBI as underreported. Asheville's NeighborhoodScout figure looked like a unit error and was converted to 800. Home values: NeighborhoodScout Q4 2025 medians. Rent: RentCafe average 2-bedroom, September 2026. Teterboro (TEB) has no usable crime, home, or rent data yet. Political lean and sports are still first-pass estimates, not checked against election results.
Borrowed crime figures: where an airport's own town has no usable FBI figure, the rate shown is borrowed from the nearby big city named in the "borrowed" tag (for example Teterboro uses New York, Hanscom uses Boston). These count toward the fit score but describe that city, not the airport's town. Crime stays blank where the FBI file has no usable number at all (GPT, JAN, NEW, ORL report zero offenses; ITO, OGG have no population figure; SJU, STT are outside the file).
Staffing, level, CPC target, entry pay and training success come from the 123ATC facilities table and refresh weekly. The date next to the title is the last successful refresh. If a value was blank at the source, the last good value is kept and tagged "stale" with the date it was last seen.
Saved scenarios store only your settings (weights, filters, compared towers, map view). Scores are recomputed from the current data each time, so a shared scenario can show different numbers than on the day it was shared.
